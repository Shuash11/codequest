Features

Everything a study session needs.

Seven connected modules, one clean desktop app — built with C# and a normalized SQLite database.

01

AI quiz generation

Pick topic, difficulty and length (5–20 questions). The provider chain authors a fresh quiz in seconds.

02

Human review gate

Generated quizzes land as drafts. Every question, answer and explanation is checked before students see it.

03

Instant scoring

Auto-scored the moment you submit — percentage, per-question verdicts, and the reasoning behind each answer.

04

Works offline

Taking quizzes, scoring, history and reports run entirely on your machine. The network is only for new questions.

05

Performance reports

Averages, best runs and a needs-practice list per topic — sorted weakest first, with topic and difficulty filters.

06

Private by design

Everything is stored in a local SQLite database on your machine. No telemetry, no accounts, no tracking.

How it works

Three steps, zero setup.

The download carries everything — app, database and configuration.

1

Install

Run the setup once — it installs into your own folder without admin rights and adds the shortcuts.

2

Run CodeQuest.exe

Sign in with a name — that's the whole login. Your attempts are saved locally from the first session.

3

Learn

Take quizzes, review every answer with explanations, then open Reports to see your weakest topics ranked.
